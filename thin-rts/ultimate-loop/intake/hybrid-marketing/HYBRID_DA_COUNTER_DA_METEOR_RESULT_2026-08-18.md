## What actually attacked the evolved loop

The useful pressure was indirect. The deck repeatedly treats stage attrition, deliberate friction, proxy qualification, and early calls-to-action as design variables. When translated into Ultimate Loop terms, four candidate gaps appeared.

### HM_EXT01 - Filter-aware stage telemetry

DA: low pass-through may be a bottleneck.

Counter-DA: a stage may intentionally exist to reject low-fit candidates. Optimizing every local conversion rate would destroy the filter.

Survivor:

`LOW THROUGHPUT != BOTTLENECK WHEN FILTER INTENT + REJECTION SEMANTICS + DOWNSTREAM QUALITY ARE EVIDENCE-BOUND`

This materially sharpens the newly surviving OSARU `EXT03_BOTTLENECK_REENTRY_ROUTING`. Re-entry should not fire before stage intent is known.

Verdict: `CANONICAL_EXTENSION_CANDIDATE`, preferably as a guard on EXT03 rather than a standalone subsystem.

### HM_EXT02 - Evidence construct validity

DA: if a gate was passed, use the result.

Counter-DA: passing a small payment gate proves only that small payment occurred/could occur. It does not prove large-purchase fit, product suitability, safety, consent, or authority. The same error class exists in software: unit-test pass does not prove deployment correctness; retrieval pass does not prove decision competence.

Survivor:

`GATE PASS MAY CLAIM ONLY THE CONSTRUCT IT MEASURED, UNLESS THE PROXY MAPPING IS SEPARATELY VALIDATED.`

Verdict: `CANONICAL_EXTENSION_CANDIDATE`.

### HM_EXT03 - Evidence-satisfied short circuit

DA: Ultimate Loop has a canonical sequence; skipping stages is dangerous.

Counter-DA: forcing a stage whose unique obligations are already satisfied or NOT_APPLICABLE wastes work and can create needless attrition. The source's repeated CTA idea exposes the general issue: a qualified case should not be forced through irrelevant steps merely because the diagram is linear.

Survivor:

`SEQUENCE ORDER != OBLIGATION DUPLICATION.`

A short circuit is eligible only when downstream preconditions and authority pass, and every remaining stage's unique evidence/safety obligation is SATISFIED or NOT_APPLICABLE.

Verdict: `STANDBY_RESEARCH`. This is potentially a major speed win, but the failure mode is too dangerous for contract-only promotion.

### HM_EXT04 - Commitment contamination guard

The source deliberately uses sunk-cost/self-justification effects as a conversion technique. That does **not** survive as doctrine.

The adversarial lesson does:

`SUNK COST != CURRENT CONSENT`

`PAST PAYMENT / TIME / CLICK / APPROVAL != CURRENT AUTHORITY OR VALIDITY`

This overlaps existing stale-approval and `CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY` rules, but adds a distinct historical-commitment contamination failure class.

Verdict: `COMPOSE_AS_HARD_INVARIANT_CANDIDATE`, likely under Human Gate/authority semantics rather than as a new lifecycle stage.
