## Surviving challenger deltas

1. **Filter-aware stage telemetry** - intentional attrition must not be misdiagnosed as a bottleneck when filter intent, rejection semantics, and downstream quality are evidence-bound.
2. **Evidence construct validity** - a gate may not claim properties it did not measure unless the proxy mapping is separately validated.
3. **Evidence-satisfied short circuit** - research-only candidate for bypassing optional stages when all unique obligations and authority are already satisfied.
4. **Commitment contamination guard** - sunk cost, old payment, time spent, clicks, or prior approval are not current consent, authority, validity, or fit.
5. **Human Gate agency preservation** - a Human Gate is not meaningful authorization if the system obscures refusal, hides consequences, preselects approval, or deliberately leans on sunk-cost/social pressure.
6. **Reference-class freeze** - changing the comparator/reference class may establish a new baseline, but cannot inherit a before/after improvement claim without paired comparable evidence.
7. **Selection lineage guard** - evidence measured after deliberate filtering/screening is evidence about the selected cohort; population-wide claims require separately validated transportability.
8. **Intervention history binding** - outcomes observed after education, repeated exposure, payment, defaults, prompts, or other interventions remain conditional on that intervention path unless causal identification supports a stronger claim.

## Support-only / rejected as universal doctrine

- named-platform preference;
- follower/reach heuristics;
- thumbnail or PASTOR/script formulas;
- deliberate sunk-cost manipulation;
- specific payment screening thresholds;
- high-ticket/middle-ticket product doctrine;
- price/revenue claims;
- authority cues as evidence of truth;
- Pareto/80:20 as permission to skip unique evidence, safety, or authority obligations;
- 3R content-reuse heuristics as canonical loop doctrine.

## Hard boundary

`MARKETING EFFECT != EVIDENCE VALIDITY`

`PERSUASION SUCCESS != CONSENT OR AUTHORITY`

`CHOICE PRESENTATION != MEANINGFUL AUTHORIZATION`

`COMPARATOR SHIFT != PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT`

`PARETO HEURISTIC != AUTHORITY TO SKIP UNIQUE OBLIGATION`

`SELECTED COHORT EVIDENCE != POPULATION EVIDENCE`

`POST INTERVENTION STATE != INTRINSIC BASELINE PROPERTY`
