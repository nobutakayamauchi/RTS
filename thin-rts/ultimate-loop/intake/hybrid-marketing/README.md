# Hybrid Marketing intake

Status: `CHALLENGER_KNOWLEDGE / NON_CANONICAL`

This intake derives general control/evidence lessons from a 143-page marketing slide deck. It does not import platform, pricing, persuasion, or sales tactics as Ultimate Loop doctrine.

All 143 pages were visually scanned. Extracted text was used only as a helper.

## Surviving challenger deltas

1. **Filter-aware stage telemetry** - intentional attrition must not be misdiagnosed as a bottleneck when filter intent, rejection semantics, and downstream quality are evidence-bound.
2. **Evidence construct validity** - a gate may not claim properties it did not measure unless the proxy mapping is separately validated.
3. **Evidence-satisfied short circuit** - research-only candidate for bypassing optional stages when all unique obligations and authority are already satisfied.
4. **Commitment contamination guard** - sunk cost, old payment, time spent, clicks, or prior approval are not current consent, authority, validity, or fit.

## Deliberately rejected as universal doctrine

- named-platform preference;
- follower/reach heuristics;
- thumbnail or script formulas;
- deliberate sunk-cost manipulation;
- specific payment screening thresholds;
- high-ticket/middle-ticket product doctrine;
- price/revenue claims;
- authority cues as evidence of truth.

## Hard boundary

`MARKETING EFFECT != EVIDENCE VALIDITY`

`PERSUASION SUCCESS != CONSENT OR AUTHORITY`

`CONTRACT METEOR PASS != CANONICAL PROMOTION`
