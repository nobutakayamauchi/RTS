## Candidate deltas exposed to Ultimate Loop

The pack proposes five things for DA / Counter-DA / METEOR testing, not automatic adoption:

1. **First-party signal binding** — distinguish direct user/operator evidence from external discovery evidence.
2. **Canonical-source compiler** — one evidence-bound canon can rebuild many replaceable adapters without factual drift.
3. **Bottleneck re-entry routing** — route a weak metric to the smallest affected gate instead of globally rewriting.
4. **Scale-proof gate** — do not multiply traffic/spend/automation before a bounded loop is proven.
5. **Decision-capability succession** — creator-independent operation requires tested reasoning/authority transfer, not only backups or reconstruction.

## DA

Strong objections retained in the pack:

- the source is business/marketing-heavy and may smuggle domain assumptions into a general development method;
- twenty operators may only be taxonomy, not an execution improvement;
- first-party customer evidence is not applicable to every workload;
- scale/profit concepts must never dominate correctness or safety;
- authority transfer may exceed the core development-loop boundary.

## Counter-DA

Current answer:

- the pack has `authority_effect = NONE`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` is permitted for workload-specific operators;
- safety and existing Ultimate Loop authority rules dominate business-efficiency claims;
- platform/price/revenue literals are mechanically rejected from universal definitions;
- only extensions that win under the same frozen workload may move toward canonical adoption.
