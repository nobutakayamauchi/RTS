# METEOR — RTS-FRZ-000018 Model Transition Intelligence + Architecture Contract Diff v1

## Survivor

Provider-neutral, repository-local comparison of immutable old/new official-document evidence bundles. Exact provenance and full-document digests are mandatory. Normalized claims are anchored to source text. Only official claims can establish a transition severity. Documentation claims remain `UNVERIFIED` behavior hypotheses until F/G measurement.

## Severity boundary

- S0: no material external contract change / marketing-only.
- S1: tuning, limit, pricing/performance, or identity change.
- S2: behavioral/interface/tool contract change.
- S3: observable execution-contract topology shift only; hidden neural architecture claim remains NONE.

## Destructive DA death

The first candidate incorrectly treated a changed document as fully mapped when any normalized claim in that document changed. A marketing-tagline claim could therefore mask a second unnormalized execution-model change elsewhere in the same README.

Death condition: `MAPPED MINOR DELTA != FULL DOCUMENT CHANGE COVERAGE`.

## Repair / Counter-DA

The survivor removes only the old/new anchors belonging to actual normalized claim deltas from each changed official document and compares the residual text. If residual text differs, H emits `REVIEW_REQUIRED` rather than claiming the transition is classified. This detects unextracted adjacent contract changes while allowing fully anchored marketing/tuning changes to remain bounded.

Counter-DA also fixed under-specified test anchors instead of weakening the production rule: claim anchors must cover the documented change they assert.
