# METEOR — FRZ-000017 Bounded Probe Executor v1

## Goal
Shorten model-generation adaptation by running F probe plans as bounded background education campaigns without converting speed into uncontrolled spend, replay, or unsafe profile application.

## DA death conditions
A candidate is dead if it silently truncates a task×probe matrix, invokes an adapter before exact fingerprint approval, ignores retry-adjusted attempt/cost ceilings, replays completed work after restart, continues across engine identity drift, persists hidden/raw model text, or applies a non-STABLE/cross-engine profile to runtime.

## Survivor
The selected implementation fails closed when the full campaign cannot fit the approved job/attempt/retry-adjusted estimated-cost budget; grants `BOUNDED_CAMPAIGN_ONLY` authority to one exact campaign fingerprint; caps parallelism at four; quarantines sanitized UNKNOWN results; stops on engine mismatch or failure/wall-clock ceilings; supports atomic persisted checkpoint/resume without replay; and emits only a human-approved rollback-capable local policy artifact for same-engine STABLE profiles. Runtime routing remains unmodified.

## Counter-DA
Persistent-worker tests prove a checkpoint can be saved to disk, loaded in a later chunk, and completed without replaying terminal jobs. A persisted checkpoint cannot be transferred to a different campaign fingerprint. During G DA, F's `max_probes=1` contract was found to return two probes; the defect was reproduced, fixed upstream in F, regression-locked, and synchronized before G completion.

## Result
SURVIVOR_SELECTED. Background education is resumable and bounded; provider-specific credentials/network adapters remain a separate future execution boundary.
