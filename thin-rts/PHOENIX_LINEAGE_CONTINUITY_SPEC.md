# Thin RTS — PHOENIX LINEAGE

Timestamp: **2026-08-11 20:26 JST**

Status: `CREATOR_INDEPENDENT_CONTINUITY_SPEC / FROZEN_FOR_INTEGRATION`

Formal name: **PHOENIX LINEAGE — Creator-Independent Continuity & Regeneration Loop**

This specification extends `MOVABLE_FRAME_DARWIN_ARENA_SPEC.md` from component replacement into long-term continuity across tool, provider, platform, schema, legal, social, and creator changes.

Its purpose is not to preserve a particular program forever.
Its purpose is to preserve the user's materially important outcomes, evidence, recovery paths, and decision memory even when the original implementation, provider, device, maintainer, or creator is gone.

## 1. Founding distinction

`HUMAN VALUE IS NOT DISPOSABLE.`

`PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATIONS ARE DISPOSABLE.`

The system may retire, replace, shrink, split, merge, externalize, or delete software components when evidence justifies doing so.

It must not transfer that disposability doctrine to people.

The human outcome is the protected objective.
The implementation is only a temporary means.

## 2. Creator-independent continuity

The system must not require the original creator to remain present in order to:

- understand why a responsibility exists;
- identify the current occupant of a Movable Frame;
- reconstruct the evidence and decision history;
- detect that an incumbent has become materially worse or obsolete;
- assemble a credible challenger from newly available external capabilities;
- replay frozen workloads and known failure classes;
- preserve or migrate canonical state;
- recover important material after loss of a provider/device/implementation;
- determine what remains UNKNOWN or requires human authority.

`CREATOR ABSENT != LINEAGE LOST`.

Creator-specific tacit knowledge is therefore a defect unless it is non-material.

## 3. Human Priority Contract

Every materially user-facing frame should bind a **Human Priority Contract** describing what must remain obtainable even if implementations change.

The contract should preserve, where relevant:

- what the user ultimately cares about;
- what event or condition makes it important;
- what evidence/state must be available at that moment;
- maximum tolerable delay where material;
- confidentiality and authority requirements;
- minimum recovery/reconstruction requirements;
- known irreversible loss windows;
- unresolved assumptions/UNKNOWN states.

The most important measure is not whether the software still runs.

It is whether the system can produce the materially important thing **when the human actually needs it**.

`SERVICE_UP != HUMAN_OUTCOME_AVAILABLE`.

## 4. Regeneration Contract

For material durable outcomes, preservation should support regeneration rather than only backup of a dead implementation.

A Regeneration Contract should identify:

- canonical source/evidence/state;
- logical schema/version;
- derivation/transformation steps;
- required external standards/tools where possible;
- verification invariants;
- export/import path;
- recovery prerequisites;
- known lossy transformations;
- authority required to regenerate or disclose the output.

Goal:

`CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION LOST`
→ `CANONICAL MATERIAL SURVIVES`
→ `NEW ENVIRONMENT / NEW TOOLCHAIN`
→ `REGENERATE REQUIRED OUTPUT`
→ `VERIFY`
→ `USE UNDER APPROPRIATE AUTHORITY`

A backup that preserves executable bytes but cannot reproduce the human-relevant outcome is not sufficient.

## 5. Era-change triggers

PHOENIX LINEAGE may reopen a frame when material environmental change appears, including:

- provider/product/API shutdown or deprecation;
- major price/terms/license change;
- operating-system/platform transition;
- file/schema/crypto format obsolescence;
- new standard or broadly superior commodity capability;
- material security/privacy change;
- law/regulation/procedure change;
- new accessibility/operator constraints;
- new hardware/runtime model;
- disappearance of a maintainer/creator;
- repeated real-world failure showing the old frame contract is no longer sufficient.

Novelty alone is not a trigger.

`ERA CHANGED != REPLACE EVERYTHING`.

The challenger must demonstrate material whole-life advantage or the incumbent must show a material uncontained defect.

## 6. Evolution direction

The system may evolve toward either smaller or larger compositions.

Smaller is preferred when the same human outcome can be achieved with lower whole-life burden.
Larger is justified only when additional responsibility materially improves the protected outcome and survives necessity/reality gates.

Therefore:

`SMALLER IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY BETTER.`

`MORE CAPABLE IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY BETTER.`

`BETTER = HIGHER VERIFIED HUMAN-OUTCOME FITNESS AT ACCEPTABLE WHOLE-LIFE BURDEN.`

## 7. Dry replacement doctrine

Software occupants have no tenure rights.

An implementation may be removed even after years of successful use when a challenger demonstrably wins the same responsibility contract.

Required replacement states may include:

- `SURVIVES`
- `SHRINKS`
- `SPLITS`
- `MERGES`
- `EXTERNALIZES`
- `RECOMPOSES`
- `RETIRES`
- `DIES`

Historical effort, authorship, affection, popularity, or age grants no automatic survival right.

But retirement must preserve material lineage, autopsy, migration evidence, and recovery obligations.

## 8. Lineage without the creator

A future maintainer, verifier, AI/toolchain, or authorized operator should be able to answer from surviving artifacts rather than private memory:

- What was this responsibility for?
- What human outcome did it protect?
- Why was this implementation selected?
- What did it replace?
- Which workloads did it survive?
- Which failures are already known?
- What state/evidence is canonical?
- How can that state be exported or regenerated?
- What authority is required for the next action?
- What is currently UNKNOWN?

If those answers require the original creator's memory, continuity is incomplete.

## 9. Succession packet

Each materially important frame should be exportable as a **Succession Packet** containing or referencing at minimum:

- frame identity and Human Priority Contract;
- current occupant/deployment identity;
- predecessor/successor lineage;
- frozen regression/reference workloads;
- known adversarial cases and autopsies;
- canonical state/evidence references;
- schema/format identities;
- regeneration procedure;
- current recovery procedure;
- secrets/key-custody requirements without exposing secrets;
- external dependencies/providers and replacement assumptions;
- authority boundaries;
- unresolved risks/UNKNOWN states.

The packet must be useful without the original AI conversation.

## 10. Phoenix test

At least one material frame should eventually survive a **Phoenix Test**:

1. assume the original creator is unavailable;
2. assume the original implementation is unavailable or intentionally removed;
3. begin only with the preserved Succession Packet, canonical material, and authorized recovery inputs;
4. reconstruct the frame's purpose and current required outcome;
5. assemble an available replacement composition without hidden creator knowledge;
6. regenerate or recover the protected human-relevant output;
7. run frozen workloads and independent verification;
8. preserve PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN and the new lineage transition.

A Phoenix Test does not grant autonomous authority for external side effects.

## 11. Creator-independent does not mean authority-free

The absence of the original creator must not become a loophole for autonomous submission, disclosure, spending, legal representation, production mutation, or other high-impact action.

The system may preserve, reconstruct, recommend, compare, prepare, and verify within authorized boundaries.

Where a required human/legal authority no longer exists or cannot be established, the correct state is `AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE / UNKNOWN`, not invented authority.

## 12. Secondary creation / by-products

Once the primary protected outcome is satisfied, the same preserved knowledge and tooling may expose secondary opportunities: reusable tools, templates, adapters, reports, automations, datasets, or other useful artifacts.

These are **by-products**, not automatic scope expansion.

Every secondary product must independently enter:

`WISH / IDEA`
→ `NECESSITY SURVIVAL`
→ `METEOR CRUCIBLE`
→ `PROMOTION IF SURVIVES`
→ `MOVABLE FRAME / DARWIN ARENA`

The core system may therefore generate useful new things without allowing those things to hijack the human-priority objective.

## 13. Relationship to Darwin Arena

DARWIN ARENA answers:

> Which occupant deserves the frame now?

PHOENIX LINEAGE adds:

> Can the frame, its purpose, its protected human outcome, and its regeneration path survive even when the original era and creator do not?

Full lifecycle:

`HUMAN NEED / WISH`
→ `NECESSITY SURVIVAL`
→ `METEOR CRUCIBLE`
→ `PROMOTED OCCUPANT`
→ `MOVABLE FRAME`
→ `REAL USE`
→ `DARWIN ARENA`
→ `REPLACE / RECOMPOSE / RETIRE`
→ `SUCCESSION PACKET + INHERITED MEMORY`
→ `ERA / CREATOR / PLATFORM CHANGE`
→ `PHOENIX TEST / REGENERATION`
→ `NEW OCCUPANT + SAME PROTECTED HUMAN OUTCOME`
→ repeat.

## 14. Final invariant

**The code may disappear. The creator may disappear. The era may change. The protected human outcome and the evidence needed to recover it must remain reconstructable for as long as the responsibility legitimately survives.**

The lineage serves the person; the programs are temporary occupants.
