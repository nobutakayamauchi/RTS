# ONE SMALL STEP v0.1 — DA / Counter-DA

Date: **2026-08-14 JST**

Status: `SURVIVES_AFTER_REVISION`

## Frozen objective

Help a person make a meaningful next step toward a better state without assuming stable capacity, correct goals, valid metrics, or already-refined experience.

The system must also be usable as a learning/training scaffold without turning evaluation into blame or human-worth scoring.

## DA attacks and revisions

### DA-01 — Wrong goal treadmill

Attack: a user can efficiently march toward a bad goal.

Revision: goals are hypotheses with `INVALIDATED` routing. Sunk effort has no authority to preserve a goal.

### DA-02 — Metric gaming / proxy failure

Attack: the chosen metric can improve while the real outcome worsens.

Revision: metric validity is separately tracked. `UNKNOWN/HYPOTHESIS` may guide experiments but cannot certify strong outcome progress.

### DA-03 — Toxic productivity

Attack: "one more step" can become a mechanism for forcing action from someone who needs to stop.

Revision: `capacity=NONE` or `do_not_push` routes to preservation/checkpoint only. Restartability is a first-class progress axis.

### DA-04 — Endless effort disguised as perseverance

Attack: a user keeps doing the same thing because no cutoff was defined.

Revision: a concrete step plan requires an expected signal, review boundary, and stop/change rule.

### DA-05 — Hallucinated failure cause

Attack: AI produces a plausible causal story and converts pain into false "learning".

Revision: cause confidence is explicit and unknown is allowed. Unknown or unrecognized cause states fail closed and route to evidence collection.

### DA-06 — Lucky success becomes doctrine

Attack: one good outcome is mistaken for a repeatable method.

Revision: success matures through measurement, explanation, personal reproduction, transfer reproduction, retained method, boundary conditions, and evidence.

### DA-07 — False Gold from a mitigation

Attack: a prevention action appears to work but the claimed root cause is still wrong.

Revision: passing prevention alone creates `FAILURE_5`; Gold Failure additionally requires verified causal evidence and retained evidence.

### DA-08 — Gold becomes dogma

Attack: once a method is marked Gold, new evidence is ignored.

Revision: contradictory evidence or regression demotes to `EXPERIENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

### DA-09 — Blamelessness becomes no accountability

Attack: "do not blame people" becomes a way to avoid responsibility.

Revision: causal analysis and accountability are separate decisions. Root-cause work is system-first; responsibility may still be assessed later with evidence/authority.

### DA-10 — Personality typing becomes discrimination

Attack: MBTI or similar labels become deterministic training routes.

Revision: typologies are non-authoritative context only. Observable learning behavior and task evidence drive guidance.

### DA-11 — Evaluation becomes oppressive overhead

Attack: every tiny task receives a full postmortem.

Revision: evaluation depth must scale with risk/irreversibility/consequence. v0 keeps the evaluator thin.

### DA-12 — Gold becomes a human ranking score

Attack: employers treat Gold count as human worth or automated hire/fire authority.

Revision: Gold describes retained method/evidence, not personal worth. v0 has no employment action/ranking authority.

### DA-13 — Partial goal failure erases growth

Attack: a failed exam/project makes all intermediate learning disappear.

Revision: progress is multi-axis. Goal outcome may fail while capability, knowledge, uncertainty, risk, or preservation progress survives.

### DA-14 — External blockers become "try harder"

Attack: missing documents, institutions, dependencies, or third parties are misdiagnosed as insufficient effort.

Revision: explicit `BLOCKER_REVIEW` considers resolve, substitute evidence, bypass, delegation, reframe, or preserve.

### DA-15 — Self-profile becomes a surveillance dossier

Attack: the orientation phase collects excessive private data.

Revision: the core has no persistence. Calling implementations must minimize data; unknown/declined fields are preferable to forced disclosure. Raw sensitive payloads are not required for the evaluator.

## Counter-DA: does strictness make the system unusable?

### Counter-DA-01 — "Gold is too hard"

Response: Gold is an endpoint, not the minimum acceptable human state. Success/Failure levels retain partial maturity instead of collapsing everything below Gold to zero.

### Counter-DA-02 — "A user cannot self-profile when lost"

Response: orientation is incremental. The next step can be simply making one part of current reality visible. The system must not demand a complete autobiography before helping.

### Counter-DA-03 — "Not every domain is reproducible"

Response: lower Success levels remain useful. Gold Success is intentionally strict and should not be granted where transfer/reproducibility cannot be shown.

### Counter-DA-04 — "Causality is often impossible to verify"

Response: then the experience remains below Gold or the prevention method is retained as bounded evidence without claiming verified cause. `UNKNOWN` is a valid output.

### Counter-DA-05 — "A stop rule kills persistence"

Response: the rule stops blind repetition, not commitment to the goal. A failed method can be changed while the higher goal survives.

### Counter-DA-06 — "The evaluator cannot know the right next step"

Response: correct. v0 does not claim that authority. It routes what kind of reasoning/evidence is needed next. AI/humans may propose concrete options externally.

## Post-revision verdict

The concept survives if kept as a **thin adaptive evaluation/guidance contract** rather than expanded into an all-knowing autonomous life coach.
