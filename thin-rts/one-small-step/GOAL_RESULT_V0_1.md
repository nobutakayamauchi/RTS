# /goal — ONE SMALL STEP v0.1

Date: **2026-08-14 JST**

Status: `THIN_EVALUATOR_SURVIVES / FULL COACHING_PLATFORM_KILLED`

## Goal

Build only what is necessary to preserve the following human-important outcome:

> A person who does not know what to do, cannot always sustain full capacity, or cannot tell whether effort is useful can establish a current state, choose one bounded meaningful step, evaluate what happened, retain reusable learning, and obtain a next step or a safe preserve/restart state.

## Discovery refresh

Adjacent patterns already exist and should be reused conceptually rather than rebuilt wholesale:

- Goblin Tools Magic ToDo decomposes difficult/stressful tasks into smaller steps.
- Google SRE formalizes blameless postmortems, evidence gathering, preventive action, and recurrence reduction.
- Duolingo uses observed learner weakness/performance to personalize subsequent practice.

These demonstrate that task decomposition, blameless failure learning, and adaptive practice are not novel responsibilities by themselves.

The surviving gap for this v0 is the **thin state/evidence contract that binds**:

`SELF PROFILE + RESOURCE MAP + REVISABLE GOAL + CHALLENGEABLE METRIC + BOUNDED STEP + MULTI-AXIS PROGRESS + SUCCESS/FAILURE EXPERIENCE REFINERY + RESTARTABILITY`

No claim is made that this is the only or globally superior implementation.

## Raison d'être destroy result

Rejected as unnecessary in RTS:

- owned LLM runtime;
- coaching marketplace;
- storage/database;
- web crawler;
- scheduler/calendar;
- communications service;
- employee scoring platform;
- medical/therapeutic layer;
- autonomous external action.

Surviving irreducible core:

- pure deterministic evaluator/router;
- explicit unknown/fail-closed states;
- experience maturity rules;
- bounded step review contract;
- destructive regression tests;
- human-readable specification/evidence records.

## Build verdict

`BUILD THIN GUIDANCE/EVIDENCE EVALUATOR`

`KILL FULL AUTONOMOUS COACHING PLATFORM`

The AI reasoning layer may remain external and replaceable. The v0 core only checks whether the reasoning/evidence state is safe enough to proceed, refine, preserve, or reopen.
